## Literature Review Structure

## Applying Established Software Engineering Practices to Agentic AI Development: Autonomy, Guardrails and Human Oversight


### 1. Introduction
- Introduce the growing use of agentic AI in software engineering
- Establish the research problem and scope of the literature review
- Explain the importance of established software engineering practices when agents perform substantial portions of the implementation
### 2. Established Software Engineeering Practices
- Examine established principles across the software development lifecycle
- Discuss requirements engineering, system design, implementation, testing and maintenance
- Establish a baseline for evaluating agentic development workflows
### 3. Agentic AI in Software Engineering
- Examine the capabilities and limiations of LLMs and autonomous software engineering agents
- Discuss how agents interact with development environments and tools
- COnsider the differences between traditional and agent-assisted software development
### 4. Structured Development Workflows and Agent Autonomy
- Investigate different approaches to agent autonomy and task delegation
- Examine structured development processes, predefined responsibilities and standard operating procedures
- Consider how workflow structure may influence development outcomes
### 5. Human Oversight, Guardrails and Code Review
- Investigate where human intervention and approval should be required
- Examine methods for restricting and monitoring agent behaviour
- Discuss reviewing, approving and integrating AI-generated code
- Consider the trade-offs between autonomy, reliability, and human effort
### 6. Agentic AI Across the software development lifecycle
- Examine how agentic AI can support requirements engineering and system design
- Discuss the risks associated with delegating higher level engineering decisions
- Consider the role of human validation, testing and quality assurance throughout development
### 7. Evaluating Agentic Software Engineering
- Review existing benchmarks, and experminetal approaches
- Examine how functional correctness, requirements satisfaction and task completion and measured
- Identify limitations of existing evaluation methods, particularly concerning process adherence and human oversight
- Consider approaches to designing and controlled experiments
### 8. Research Gaps and Conclusions
- Synthesise the findings and limitations of existing research
- Identify areas where further investigation is needed
- Consider whether existing evaluations adequately address established software engineering practices
- Explain how the literature informs the proposed research methodology and agentic development workflow
